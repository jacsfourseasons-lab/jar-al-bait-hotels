Empowering Saudi Talent and Communities

At Red Sea Global, people are at the heart of every destination we create. We’re building meaningful careers for Saudi talent and supporting local communities through education, training, and enterprise.

Our goal is to ensure the growth of The Red Sea and AMAALA delivers lasting social and economic value – empowering individuals, families, and entrepreneurs along the Kingdom’s west coast and beyond.

Sustainable Social Impact

RSG’s annual Social Development Bulletin captures our progress in creating meaningful social and economic value for communities surrounding our destinations.

 

First published in 2024, the bulletin documents how our regenerative approach extends beyond the natural environment: RSG is also creating opportunities for people and communities to thrive alongside our destinations.

Through various initiatives and partnerships, each edition showcases our contribution to socioeconomic empowerment, community wellbeing, and sustainable economic growth. We’re enhancing the region’s quality of life and fostering long-term community prosperity by connecting local talent with employment and training opportunities, supporting local businesses and artisans, and celebrating cultural heritage.

Our commitment is to leave places better, creating a lasting impact for people, the economy, and beyond our borders.
