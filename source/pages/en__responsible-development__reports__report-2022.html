2022

Key Highlights

Environmental Stewardship

 

Conducted one of the largest environmental studies of wildlife in The Red Sea area, enhancing conservation efforts and setting new standards for responsible tourism.

Sustainability Certification

 

Achieved ISO14001:2015 Environmental Management System certification, ensuring our operations meet rigorous environmental standards.

Community Engagement

 

Launched Souq Amerah, supporting local entrepreneurs and attracting over 50,000 visitors, fostering community development.

Governance Excellence

 

Became the first in Saudi Arabia to achieve ISO37000:2021 for good governance, demonstrating our leadership in responsible development.

Looking Ahead

 

As we move forward, RSG remains committed to expanding its sustainability initiatives.

 

Our goals for 2023 and beyond include increasing renewable energy capacity, enhancing waste management practices, and furthering our community development programs. We are dedicated to setting new benchmarks in regenerative tourism and sustainability.
