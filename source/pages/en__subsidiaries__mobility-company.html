Coastal Transportation Services Company

Redefining guest mobility through seamless land, air, and marine transportation 

Overview

Coastal Transportation Services Company delivers integrated and sustainable mobility solutions across land, air, and marine networks within Red Sea Global destinations. The company ensures that every journey – whether by car, plane, or boat – is safe, efficient, and memorable for guests and staff alike.

 

Established to redefine mobility at The Red Sea, the company combines strategic planning with advanced technology and sustainable infrastructure development. Its scope covers transportation operations, maintenance, and marina management, contributing to a connected, world-class destination experience.

From guest transfers to marina logistics, Coastal Transportation Services Company supports the operational and commercial success of Red Sea Global by enhancing mobility reliability, efficiency, and design excellence.
