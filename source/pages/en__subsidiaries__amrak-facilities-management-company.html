Amrak Facilities Management Company

Delivering world-class facilities management services that uphold Red Sea Global’s standards of quality

Overview

Amrak Facilities Management Company (FMC) was established to enhance control over service delivery standards and ensure the highest levels of operational quality across Red Sea Global destinations. 

Amrak’s mission is to build and maintain a highly skilled workforce capable of operating and servicing RSG’s extensive facilities portfolio – from hospitality and corporate buildings to coastal and residential environments. Its integrated approach covers housekeeping, maintenance, waste management, and landscaping, ensuring every property operates to world-class standards. 

 

Through rigorous training, sustainable practices, and consistent monitoring, Amrak supports the long-term upkeep of The Red Sea and AMAALA destinations while maintaining alignment with Red Sea Global’s brand identity and operational excellence.

Key Responsibilities & Services

Oversee FMC Operations

 

Manage the creation and day-to-day operations of the in-house Facilities Management Company, ensuring the efficient delivery of services across Red Sea Global facilities. 

Ensure Workforce Competency

 

Recruit, train, and retain a skilled workforce capable of maintaining facilities, including housekeeping, maintenance, waste management, and landscape upkeep. 

Manage Service Delivery

 

Supervise key facility services such as façade cleaning, beach maintenance, golf cart operations, lifeguard services, catering, and laundry to ensure consistent quality standards.

Monitor and Enforce Standards

 

Evaluate performance regularly, implement improvements, and ensure all services align with RSG’s operational benchmarks and sustainability goals.

Housekeeping

 

Cleaning and janitorial services for corporate spaces and accommodation facilities. 

Façade Cleaning

 

High-level façade and external cleaning using sustainable methods. 

Catering & Laundry

 

Full-service catering and laundry operations for staff and guest facilities. 

MEP & Civil Maintenance

 

Preventative and corrective maintenance of mechanical, electrical, and plumbing systems. 

Beach Cleaning & Maintenance

 

Shoreline cleaning, sand nourishment, and environmental upkeep. 

Waste Management

 

Collection, segregation, and transport of waste using sustainable methods. 

Golf Cart Operations

 

Operation and upkeep of golf carts across destination assets.

Lifeguard Services

 

Professional lifeguard deployment ensuring guest safety across beach and pool areas. 

Landscaping Maintenance

 

Maintenance and enhancement of landscaped areas and outdoor environments. 

Specialist Contractors (Outsourced)

 

Oversight of specialist systems such as firefighting, elevators, CCTV, and pest control.
