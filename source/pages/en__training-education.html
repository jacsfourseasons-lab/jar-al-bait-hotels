OVERVIEW

Where Talent Becomes Legacy

At Red Sea Global, we believe that our greatest investment is in people. As a pioneering developer of regenerative tourism destinations, our mission extends beyond landscapes and infrastructure, it includes building up young Saudis to grow into leaders of tomorrow. 

Through targeted training programs, strategic educational partnerships, and initiatives aligned with Saudi Arabia’s Vision 2030, we are creating opportunities for a future-ready workforce to thrive. From vocational training to world-class hospitality and sustainability education, we are empowering the next generation of leaders who will drive innovation, environmental stewardship, and excellence across our projects and destinations. 

 

Because building the world’s most ambitious destinations starts with developing extraordinary people.

The RSG Education Framework

How RSG’s Education Mandate Is Changing the Game

The RSG Education Framework is a first-of-its-kind national model that provides global academic excellence in the field of luxury hospitality and tourism, and connects to Saudi Arabia’s job market needs, creating real-world employability for more than 1,000 local talents and future generations. 

 

The framework provides a 2.5-year learning pathway, guiding students from English foundations to internationally accredited vocational programs, hands-on internships, and guaranteed career opportunities across our destinations.

Explore the Full Depth of RSG’s Education Framework

The full RSG Education Framework illuminates how we prepare the Kingdom’s future leaders in hospitality, tourism, and regenerative development. 

 

By embedding education within its regenerative development mission, RSG is not only building destinations; it is building generations capable of sustaining them.
