Overview

The Art of Island Living

Red Sea Residences represents a new standard in ultra-luxury coastal living, offering the rare opportunity to own a home at one of the world’s most extraordinary island environments within The Red Sea destination on the west coast of Saudi Arabia.

 

Developed and curated by Red Sea Global, these limited-edition residences reflect the company’s vision for regenerative tourism, where development enhances natural environments and enriches local communities.

 

Each home is crafted in partnership with world-renowned architects and hospitality brands to deliver a lifestyle defined by privacy, design excellence, and proximity to nature.

Red Sea Residences Brand

Launched in 2025, Red Sea Residences unites RSG’s design, development, and hospitality expertise under a single brand dedicated to bespoke homes. 

 

Each property embodies quality, craftsmanship, and architectural elegance — designed for discerning buyers seeking a rare, secure, and meaningful connection to place.

Shura Residences

The first Red Sea Residences are located on Shura Island, the central hub of The Red Sea destination. Set among 11 luxury resorts and overlooking the island’s marina and golf course, the 305 planned homes include private villas and branded apartments.

Laheq Island

Announced in 2025, Laheq Island is the next evolution of luxury residential development at The Red Sea. Conceived as the destination’s first residential-led island, Laheq introduces the concept of the Forever Garden – a living landscape where homes, hospitality, and nature exist in harmony. 

 

At its heart lies The Ring, an 800-meter-wide architectural centerpiece designed by Foster + Partners, encircling a lagoon and white-sand beaches. It integrates apartments, two hotels, a souk, and private residences within a single sculptural form. 

 

Laheq Island will feature a curated mix of luxury villas, apartments, and branded residences, offering panoramic views, direct water access, and proximity to a marina, golf course, and wellness facilities.
