Fly Red Sea

Above all experiences – making the Red Sea as memorable from above as it is on the ground 

Overview 

From Red Sea Calm To Open Sky

Fly Red Sea is the Kingdom’s first seaplane company, established to connect guests seamlessly to island and coastal destinations across The Red Sea and AMAALA. It provides air transportation services from Red Sea International and Al Wajh Airports to resorts, ensuring safe, efficient, and premium guest transfers.

 

As a fully licensed aviation entity, Fly Red Sea operates under the General Authority of Civil Aviation (GACA) and is VAT-registered, holding multiple operational and aerodrome certifications. The company also manages scenic aerial tours and in-house seaplane maintenance, ensuring reliability and world-class service standards.

Through its focus on performance, safety, and customer experience, Fly Red Sea enhances access to Red Sea Global’s destinations while contributing to Saudi Arabia’s growing air mobility ecosystem.
