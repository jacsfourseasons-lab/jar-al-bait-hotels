Supply Chain & Logistics Company

Streamlining supply chains through consolidated demand and value-driven logistics solutions 

Overview

The Supply Chain & Logistics Company serves as RSG’s centralized purchasing, warehousing, logistics, and distribution entity. Its mandate is to streamline procurement, aggregate demand, and enhance supply chain efficiency.

 

The company provides end-to-end logistics services, including international freight, customs clearance, warehousing, and last-mile delivery, ensuring continuity of operations across RSG’s destinations.

Key Responsibilities & Services

Centralized Trading

 

Act as a centralized purchasing organization, connecting RSG customers with global and local supplier networks. 

Streamlined Operations

 

Optimize purchasing, warehousing, logistics, and transportation processes to improve efficiency and cost-effectiveness.

Value Optimization

 

Enhance the RSG ecosystem by optimizing spend, improving product quality, and expanding the range of available goods and services. 

Responsive Logistics

 

Deliver sustainable, reliable, and timely third-party logistics (3PL) services to ensure the continuity of operations across RSG destinations. 

Transport & Distribution (Logistics)

 

Movement of goods across the RSG supply network, including long-haul and last-mile delivery. 

Storage & Handling (Logistics)

 

Management of goods in designated warehouses, including secure handling and inventory oversight. 

International Freight & Customs Clearance (Logistics)

 

Coordination of international shipments, ensuring smooth port entry and customs processing. 

Control Tower (Logistics)

 

End-to-end tracking of supply chain operations with risk management, reporting, and real-time insights. 

Strategic Sourcing (Trading)

 

Identification and onboarding of suppliers, sustainable sourcing, and contract management. 

Purchasing as a Service (Trading)

 

Execution of procurement activities, ensuring goods availability across all RSG entities. 

Distribution (Trading)

 

Stocking and delivery of goods based on forecasted demand to support operational readiness.
