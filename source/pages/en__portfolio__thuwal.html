Overview

Where Exclusivity Meets Luxury

Thuwal Private Retreat is a boutique island project developed, owned, and operated by Red Sea Global, reflecting the company’s ability to deliver exceptional, world-class hospitality experiences. 

Located within a pristine coral archipelago off the coast of Jeddah, Thuwal is the only private island in the Red Sea available for exclusive use. The 1.7-hectare retreat offers complete seclusion and the luxury of privacy, blending Arabian elegance with contemporary design and meticulous attention to detail. 

Accessible via a 45-minute private yacht transfer from KAUST North Marina, Thuwal demonstrates Red Sea Global’s expertise in developing and operating highly personalized hospitality projects in remote, coastal environments. 

A Personalized Escape

Development & Design

Red Sea Global led every stage of Thuwal’s development, from master planning and construction to interior design and operations. The retreat’s architecture and layout emphasize privacy, comfort, and seamless indoor–outdoor living, complemented by panoramic views of the Red Sea. 

 

The island features a three-bedroom master villa, three one-bedroom pavilions, a spa villa, and multiple indoor and outdoor dining areas surrounded by private beaches and clear blue waters. Thuwal’s design integrates craftsmanship, natural materials, and understated elegance to create a warm and inviting atmosphere worthy of its exclusive clientele.

Guest Experience

Exclusive, Tailored Island Experience

Thuwal Private Retreat operates as a buy-out-only property, welcoming just one group at a time. Guests enjoy full access to the island’s facilities, supported by a dedicated on-island team led by a personal manager. 

 

Each stay is individually tailored – from water sports and culinary experiences to spa treatments and leisure activities. Every moment is curated to deliver the luxury of complete privacy and a level of service that reflects Red Sea Global’s hallmark for excellence.

Local Impact

Championing Local Talent & Community Growth

Thuwal reflects Red Sea Global’s dedication to Saudi talent development and local collaboration. The project prioritizes hiring from nearby communities and sourcing ingredients from local producers wherever possible. 

 

These efforts help build local capability in hospitality management, culinary arts, and operations, while contributing to the economic and professional growth of the western Saudi coastline.
