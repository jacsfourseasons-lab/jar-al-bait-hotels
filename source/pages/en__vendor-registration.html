Championing Local Content in Saudi Arabia

RSG is deeply committed to fostering local content as a cornerstone of Saudi Arabia’s Vision 2030, driving economic diversification and empowering the Kingdom’s workforce and industries. By prioritizing partnerships with Saudi-based suppliers, RSG ensures that a significant portion of our spend remains within the local economy, supporting job creation and skills development.By embedding local content requirements into our key processes, RSG creates lasting opportunities for small and medium enterprises (SMEs) and builds a resilient supply chain that aligns with national priorities for sustainable growth and self-reliance.

Suppliers have a duty and personal commitment to uphold our common ideals, as set out in our Code of Conduct, which enables us to continue to prosper while keeping our reputation of a high standard of integrity and ethics.

 

The Code of Conduct contains policies that relate to the legal and ethical standards of conduct that suppliers are expected to comply with while carrying out their fiduciary duties and responsibilities to us.

 

This Code is intended to help suppliers focus on areas of ethical risk, provide guidance to recognize and deal with ethical issues, provide mechanisms to report unethical conduct, and help foster a culture of honesty and accountability.

 

As part of a vendor’s registration process, we require you to sign and return the Code.

 

Please note: pre-qualification is not a guarantee of future business with RSG.

To become an RSG supplier, the first step is to register your interest by completing the “Register Your Interest” form. RSG continually reviews the profiles of potential suppliers to identify potential opportunities for them to participate in. Where we do find an opportunity that matches your capabilities, we will reach out to you to progress with a more formal process to register your company as a Supplier.

It takes just a few minutes to Register Your Interest for RSG review. If we contact you to complete formal registration it will typically take approximately one week.

Registered and qualified companies automatically receive tenders for their specific category.

Registered suppliers gain access to RSG’s planned procurement pipeline. Be aware that this pipeline may change.

A physical presence in Saudi Arabia is not always necessary, depending on the service provided. However, having an agent or office in the Kingdom could be advantageous. There's no requirement for potential partners to be partly or fully owned by a Saudi national or entity.

 

RSG welcomes companies from around the world, including those without prior experience in the Kingdom or region. However, international experience is preferred.

While materials produced in the Kingdom are preferable, RSG recognizes that some products, equipment, and materials can only be produced outside the region.

RSG does not charge fees to receive or participate in its tenders. In most cases, vendors are not required to submit tender bonds.
