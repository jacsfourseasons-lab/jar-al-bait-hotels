OVERVIEW

Rethinking the Future of Travel

Tourism connects people, cultures, and landscapes, but it can also leave a lasting footprint. At Red Sea Global, we believe travel should restore more than it takes. 

Regenerative tourism reimagines the purpose of travel as an act of renewal, where every visitor contributes to the health of the environment and the well-being of local communities.  It’s not just a concept for us – it’s how we design, build, and operate. Through innovation, data-driven planning, and community partnerships, we are proving that development and regeneration can thrive together.

Our Blueprint for Regeneration

The Regenerative Tourism Blueprint is the foundation of Red Sea Global’s approach – a measurable framework that defines how we design, construct, and operate destinations that deliver a positive net impact. 

 

It aligns environmental science, community development, and economic resilience into one model that others can adopt worldwide. 

 

Through this framework, we aim to achieve a 30% net conservation gain across our destinations by 2040, ensuring that nature and society emerge stronger with every step of progress.

The Three Pillars of Regenerative Tourism

Environment

We protect and enhance fragile ecosystems through coral relocation, mangrove planting, seagrass restoration, and 100% renewable energy systems that power every destination.

Community

We empower people through education, local employment, and cultural preservation, creating opportunities that sustain livelihoods and honour Saudi heritage. 

Economy

We’re building an economy that uplifts communities, restores nature, empowers local suppliers, and sets a new global benchmark for regenerative tourism.
