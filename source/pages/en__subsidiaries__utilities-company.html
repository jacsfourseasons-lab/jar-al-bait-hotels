Overview

Red Sea Utilities Company provides integrated utility solutions across Red Sea Global destinations, ensuring reliable, innovative, and sustainable infrastructure services. The company manages advanced field technologies – including metering systems, ICT assets, and EV chargers – while delivering efficient operations, maintenance, and customer service. 

With dedicated expertise in technical services and commercial strategy, Red Sea Utilities oversees project delivery, concession agreements, financial reporting, and customer billing. Through its holistic approach, the company ensures utility services meet the highest standards of performance, innovation, and long-term sustainability.
