Overview

Red Sea Security provides comprehensive security operations across RSG assets, ensuring a controlled and safe environment for guests, employees, and infrastructure. The company delivers standardized safety protocols, trained personnel, and integrated surveillance and access systems.

Its operational model emphasizes scalability and compliance, enabling it to support complex environments such as airports, resorts, coastal communities, and remote islands. Red Sea Security ensures security measures remain guest-centric, discreet, and aligned with luxury service expectations.
