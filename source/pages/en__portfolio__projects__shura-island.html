Shura Island – The Heart of The Red Sea

11 luxury resorts, 305 residences, Saudi Arabia’s first island golf course

Overview

Shura Island is the centerpiece of The Red Sea destination. Designed by Foster + Partners under the Coral Bloom concept, the island integrates ultra-luxury resorts, residences, and leisure offerings with regenerative design and world-class sustainability standards. 

 

By the end of 2025, Shura will open with three resorts – SLS The Red Sea, InterContinental The Red Sea, and The Red Sea EDITION – plus Shura Links, Saudi Arabia’s first island golf course. Eight further resorts will follow in 2026, alongside 305 Red Sea Residences.

Shura Links

Shura Links is Saudi Arabia’s first 18-hole island golf course, designed by Brian Curley and managed by Golf Saudi.

 

With waterfront holes, sweeping marina views, and a LEED Platinum clubhouse by Foster + Partners, it is among the most sustainable golf courses in the region. 

 

  • Only 20% of the 140-hectare course is maintained turf 
  • Irrigated with recycled wastewater 
  • Supported by POGO soil moisture sensors to minimize water use

Design & Sustainability

The Coral Bloom concept by Foster + Partners ensures Shura blends with its natural surroundings through lightweight structures, native landscaping, and minimal impact construction. 

 

Shura Island is powered by renewable energy from The Red Sea’s solar farms, supported by one of the world’s largest off-grid battery storage systems. All resorts are targeting LEED Platinum certification, contributing to RSG’s goal of delivering a 30% net conservation benefit by 2040. 

Access & Timeline

Getting Here: Guests arrive through Red Sea International Airport (RSI), served by domestic and international flights. From there, a 25-minute journey across Saudi Arabia’s longest over-water bridge brings them to Shura Island.
