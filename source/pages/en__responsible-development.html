Responsible Development

Creating opportunities for people and protecting nature as we build a thriving, sustainable economy.

Building Prosperity with Purpose

At Red Sea Global, responsible development is about ensuring progress benefits, including people, nature, and the economy alike.

We’re shaping destinations that create jobs, grow local businesses, and protect the unique environments that make them possible. From sustainable design to community engagement, every decision we make reflects a balance between economic growth and environmental care.

Our Pillars of Sustainability

At Red Sea Global, sustainability is the foundation on which every decision is made. From protecting fragile ecosystems and operating on clean energy, to empowering local communities and setting new benchmarks for responsible tourism, our approach is structured around four interconnected pillars that guide how we plan, build, and operate.

AIR, LAND AND SEA

Environmental Stewardship


We protect and restore the natural environment across air, land, and sea. Continuous monitoring systems, habitat restoration programs, and science-led planning ensure our destinations contribute to measurable conservation gains while preserving the pristine character of the Red Sea coast.

Sustainable Operations

Clean Energy & Resource Efficiency



Our operations are designed to run on renewable energy and optimize resource use. Large-scale solar farms, advanced battery storage, water stewardship, and waste management systems enable our destinations to operate with minimal environmental footprint.

Purpose-Driven Development

People & Community Empowerment


We invest in people as much as places. Through education, vocational training, and employment pathways, we create opportunities for local communities and future generations to participate in the growth of regenerative tourism.

Governance & Accountability

Responsible Development & Governance


Sustainability is embedded into our governance, procurement, design standards, and operational models. Clear policies, international benchmarks, and transparent reporting ensure accountability at every stage of the project lifecycle.

Grounded in regeneration

Protecting and Enhancing the Natural Environment

We take a science-led approach to conservation, using marine spatial planning and data from thousands of environmental sensors to guide where and how we build. This ensures that development enhances biodiversity – from coral reefs and seagrass meadows to mangrove forests.

 

These efforts contribute to our wider commitment to regenerative tourism, aiming to achieve a 30% net conservation gain across our destinations by 2040.
