OVERVIEW

Shaping RSG’s Hospitality Ecosystem

Red Sea Global Hospitality serves as RSG’s dedicated hospitality arm, responsible for the operation and management of hotels, resorts, serviced residences, and destination dining assets. The company is instrumental in embedding a structured service culture rooted in Saudi hospitality values while aligning with global operational standards. 

 

Through focused talent development initiatives, it supports national workforce growth and ensures local talent is developed to meet the needs of the luxury hospitality sector. With a growing portfolio of flagship assets, the company is positioned to become a regional and international operator of reference.

FAQS

Red Sea Global Hospitality manages and operates luxury resorts and residences across RSG’s destinations. Its flagship portfolio includes Shebara and Desert Rock at The Red Sea destination, Turtle Bay Village for staff and families, and Thuwal Private Retreat, located further south near Jeddah.

While it was established to manage properties within The Red Sea and AMAALA, the company is expanding its operations to support other luxury and lifestyle destinations across the Kingdom and the wider region.

The company operates under RSG’s integrated developer-operator model, combining development expertise, brand alignment, and day-to-day management. This ensures consistency across all properties and strengthens Saudi Arabia’s domestic hospitality capability.

Red Sea Global Hospitality currently manages the Shebara and Desert Rock resorts as flagship luxury destinations, Turtle Bay Village as a residential and community hub for staff and families, and Thuwal Private Retreat near Jeddah as an exclusive coastal experience.

By training Saudi talent, introducing new hospitality standards, and operating luxury destinations that attract international tourism, the company supports Vision 2030’s goals for economic diversification and national employment.
