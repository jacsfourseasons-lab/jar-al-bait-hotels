Towards a Promising Tomorrow

Overview

Red Sea Global (RSG), in collaboration with the Human Resources Development Fund (HRDF), is offering the sixth edition of the Red Sea Vocational Training Program as part of its efforts to prepare young Saudis for promising professional tracks that align with the needs of its growing tourism destinations.

Upon successfully completing the program, which runs for two years and four months, students obtain a diploma degree, followed by a job opportunity at Red Sea Global's destinations, one of its subsidiaries, or companies operating in any region of the Kingdom.

Program Objectives

Empower national talent

Enable young Saudis to contribute to the development of world-class tourism destinations.

Educational programs aligned with the labor market

Equip young Saudis with the skills and knowledge that align with the job requirements at Red Sea Global's destinations.

Distinctive career tracks

Strengthen tourism-sector specializations in line with the operational needs of the company's destinations.

Admission Criteria for Phase I (English Foundation Program)

Terms and Conditions (for all tracks)

 Admission Criteria of Phase I (English Program)

 

Applicants must:

Be a Saudi national and possess a valid national identity. 

Must be 18 years or older. 

Must not have a commercial record under the Ministry of Commerce. 

Must be registered in the National Labor Portal "TAQAT". 

Applicants must be fully dedicated to their studies and must not be enrolled as a student at any educational institution inside or outside the Kingdom.

Applicants must not currently be employed in the public or private sector and must not be registered with social insurance (GOSI).

Must not have previously benefited from Human Resources Development Fund (HRDF) support.

Must not have previously benefited from RSG educational programs. 

Applicants must pass a medical examination after initial acceptance to confirm their health and physical fitness to work in the chosen field.

Applicants must hold a general secondary school (high school) certificate as a minimum and achieve a score of no less than 60% in the General Aptitude Test.

Submit all required documents during the registration stage. 

Sign a pledge to commit to working at one of Red Sea Global's destinations or its companies for no less than 3 years after graduation.

Phase II 

Admission Criteria (Vocational Training Program)

Upon successful completion of Phase I (the English Foundation Program), students undergo a phased assessment to determine their eligibility to join the Vocational Training Program. Applicants who meet the criteria will be nominated to begin this phase.

Phase III

Admission Criteria (Internship)

Upon completing the Vocational Training Program, students undergo a final assessment as a prerequisite for joining the on-the-job training phase.

Additional Conditions for the Tourism Security Track

  • Height must be proportionate to weight, no less than 165 cm for male applicants and 160 cm for female applicants.
  • Age: 18 to 25 years
  • The physical fitness required for the role of security officer
  • Readiness to work different shifts: morning, evening, and night
  • Readiness to work on weekends and official holidays
  • Readiness and ability to work in any city according to the requirements of the role
  • Clean criminal background certificate

Important Notes

Accepted students must take English Proficiency standarazied test (STEP, IELTS,  or equivalent) and submit the following minimum scores at three key stages of the program:

 

  • Minimum score of 52 in STEP, 3.0 in IELTS, or equivalent score in other standardized tests must be submitted by December 17th, 2026.
  • Minumum score of 56 in STEP, 3.5 in IELTS, or equivalent score in other standardized tests must be submitted by July 29th, 2027.
  • Minimum score of 60 in STEP, 4.0 in IELTS, or equivalent score in other standardized tests must be submitted by January 27th, 2028.

 

All of the above scores must be submitted to schools on the mentioned dates above. Only students who achieve the above scores are eligible to complete the program.

How to Apply

1

Apply on RSG Education Platform

  • Applicants can apply to one program or more.
  • All required documents must be uploaded as instructed, and the application will not be considered if any required information or document is missing or incorrect.
  • Please make sure all information is correct before submitting your application.
  • Registration opens on July 5, 2026 and closes on July 11, 2026.

2

Reviewing and Filtration 

  • All completed applications will be reviewed carefully.
  • Applicants will be invited to in-person interviews after meeting all conditions and criteria and submitting all required documents.

3

Language Placement Test

Qualified applicants will be invited to take an English language placement test as part of the admission process.

4

Interviews

Top-ranked candidates will be invited for interviews; all interviews will be conducted face-to-face. Further details on dates and locations will be shared once candidates are selected.
