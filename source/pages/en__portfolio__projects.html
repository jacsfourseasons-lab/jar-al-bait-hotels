Red Sea International Airport

Designed by Foster + Partners, the airport’s dune-inspired terminal reflects the natural beauty of its surroundings and sets new benchmarks for design excellence in the region. The facility is powered entirely by renewable energy, in line with Red Sea Global’s commitment to regenerative development. 

Blending architectural innovation with environmental responsibility, RSI offers travelers a luxurious and seamless arrival experience while championing sustainable aviation.

Desert Rock Resort

Six Senses Southern Dunes

Built from local stone and timber and powered entirely by solar energy, the property features grey-water recycling and zero-waste systems. Six Senses Southern Dunes stands as RSG’s first fully operational model of regenerative tourism in practice.
