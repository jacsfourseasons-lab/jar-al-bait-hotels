Diversity, equity & inclusion

Our commitment to gender diversity reflects wider changes in Saudi Arabia as a whole

With the country already surpassing its Vision 2030 target for women to make up 30% of the workforce, Red Sea Global is an inclusive employer that offers equal opportunities for men and women from the Kingdom, as well as international citizens who bring the right expertise and attitude into our fold.

HAYAH Wellbeing Program

We recognize the importance of our employees’ mental, physical, and workplace-related wellbeing as a key pillar of our commitment to employee health.

 

Named HAYAH (meaning ‘life’ in Arabic), our holistic Colleague Wellbeing Program fosters a culture of wellness across our Group.

 

HAYAH offers a range of initiatives designed to support the health needs of over 4,500 employees, using a data-driven, evidence-based approach to:  

Drive our culture of workplace wellbeing

Provide opportunities for physical health

Support employees' mental health
