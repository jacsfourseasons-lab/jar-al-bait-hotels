Shaping a Better Future for People and Planet

Red Sea Global (RSG) is a vertically integrated real estate developer with a diverse portfolio spanning tourism, residential, experiences, infrastructure, transport, healthcare, and services.

 

As a Public Investment Fund (PIF) company, RSG is guided by Saudi Arabia’s Vision 2030 to create destinations and projects that deliver lasting social, environmental, and economic value ­– building opportunities for people while protecting the planet.

 

From our flagship destinations, The Red Sea and AMAALA, to a growing portfolio of developments including Thuwal Private Retreat, the Al Wajh Airport upgrade, and international projects, we are redefining how destinations are designed, built, and operated. Supported by a network of subsidiaries, our integrated approach enables us to create extraordinary places that generate long-term value for the Kingdom's economy, its communities and natural environment.
