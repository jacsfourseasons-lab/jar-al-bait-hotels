Overview

A New Era of Luxury Living in Harmony with Nature

AMAALA Residences represents a new era of luxury wellness living, offering discerning homeowners the chance to own a property in one of the world’s most anticipated destinations. 

Nestled between the Hijaz Mountains and the crystal waters of the Red Sea, these homes are designed by Red Sea Global to combine architectural excellence with a deep connection to nature. Every detail is guided by AMAALA’s commitment to wellbeing, creativity, and regeneration – creating a community where life flourishes in harmony with the environment. 

Launching alongside the first AMAALA resorts at Triple Bay in 2026, AMAALA Residences introduces a limited collection of branded homes that redefine holistic coastal living.

Branded Residences

The Collection

Clinique La Prairie Residences

 

Designed by acclaimed architect John Heah, the Clinique La Prairie Residences comprise 13 exceptional homes nestled within a serene oasis. These are the first ever residences available for purchase from the world-renowned Swiss longevity clinic. 

Each residence combines luxury with medical-grade wellness, providing direct access to Clinique La Prairie’s Health Resort and nearly a century of expertise in longevity, nutrition, and holistic care – a rare opportunity to live better, longer. 

Nammos Residences

 

Inspired by the lifestyle of the Aegean, Nammos Residences introduces 20 spacious apartments and penthouses ranging from one to three bedrooms. Interiors reflect the vivid blues and seagrass greens of the Mediterranean, with warm timber millwork and handcrafted terrazzo detailing. 

 

Residents enjoy the signature Nammos blend of relaxed sophistication, privacy, and beach-club vibrancy – a first for the Red Sea. 

Rosewood Residences & Iconic Villa

Rosewood Residences AMAALA features 26 three- to five-bedroom villas designed by Antonio Citterio Patricia Viel (ACPV). Each home blends indoor and outdoor living through soft geometry, natural textures, and panoramic sea views. 

 

The Andar Club Residences

 

Set between the Hijaz Mountains and the Red Sea, The Andar Club Residences form a community of three- to six-bedroom homes overlooking a nine-hole golf course and a private clubhouse. 

Inspired by the world’s most prestigious country clubs, The Andar Club offers an abundance of lifestyle amenities including a Wellness Route, recreational paths, and exclusive access to AMAALA’s golf, wellness, and cultural programs.
