English for Tourism

Bridging Local Communities with Global Hospitality 

Overview

As part of Red Sea Global’s (RSG) ongoing commitment to community development, the English for Tourism Program launches its third iteration,  upskilling local community members and preparing them for future career opportunities within the tourism and hospitality sectors. 

Over the course of six months, this online general English training equips participants with essential language skills, enhancing their employability in the growing tourism industry.  

More than 3,000 people have benefited from the program to date, gaining valuable knowledge and exposure to employment opportunities within RSG’s destinations. 

Interested candidates can register through the Jewar App, which allows residents of the Red Sea region to apply for training programs, explore job opportunities, and share suggestions directly with the RSG team. This initiative reflects our broader mission to empower local communities through education and capacity building, fostering a sustainable and prosperous future for the people surrounding our destinations.  

Learning Objectives

This training program contributes to enhancing participants’ language skills, strengthening their readiness to engage in the rapidly growing tourism sector within Red Sea Global destinations. Through the Jiwar App, local communities are provided with learning and empowerment pathways that support a more prosperous and sustainable future along the Red Sea coast.

Linguistics Skills

Build essential English language skills and the confidence needed to communicate effectively with the Kingdom’s international visitors.

Opportunities

Unlock new, long-term, and rewarding career paths in tourism and hospitality.

Professional Development

Develop strong speaking, writing, and professional communication skills that are essential for success in today’s competitive job market.

ELIGIBILITY

Admission Requirements

Nationality

Open to Saudi nationals only & Possess a valid national ID. 

Local Residents

Residents from Umluj, Al Wajh, Duba, Al-Ais or surrounding villages. 

Age Limit

Applicants must be 16 to 30 years old. 

Training Completion

Participants must complete the training and maintain a minimum of 80% attendance to receive a certificate. 

New Horizons

The English for Tourism program enables young Saudis from local communities to learn the fundamentals of English and refine their communication skills, empowering them to actively contribute to their region’s development and succeed in the Kingdom’s tourism industry.
