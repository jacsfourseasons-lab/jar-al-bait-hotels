Elite Graduate Program

Dynamic on the job-training designed to prepare Saudi fresh graduates for future careers at Red Sea Global

Jobs for the Future

Our Elite Graduate Program, entering its sixth edition, enrolls fresh Saudi graduates through a comprehensive system of placement, training programs, and encouraging individuals to achieve their highest potential.

Broaden your experience and enhance your potential through our designated mentoring, coaching, and shadowing programs.

Objectives

The program provides Saudi graduates with entry-level jobs which equip them with hands-on experience in exciting roles. 

Opportunities

Create job opportunities for Saudi youth 

Training

Prepare fresh graduates through on-the-job training 

Transfer Knowledge

Transfer knowledge in new fields by working with international experts 

Targeted Streams

Finance and Investment

The Finance and Investment team are responsible for driving long-term strategic value by planning, implementing, managing, and controlling all financial and investment related activity of the Company.

 

This includes disciplines in feasibility, investment, finance, accounting, asset management, investor relations, and insurance.

Required Majors: BS in Finance, Real Estate, and Hospitality.

Projects Delivery

The Project Delivery stream oversees planning, design, engineering, and construction of all assets. It is responsible for the development of all processes and procedures which underpin the project, design and construction management practices adopted by RSG.

 

As an Elite in this department, you will experience how projects are established, designed, and successfully delivered working with multidisciplinary teams.

Required Majors: Construction Management, Civil Engineering, Structural Engineering, Building Engineering, Architecture, Landscape Architecture, Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Interior Design. 

Technical Project Management Office

The TPMO department is responsible for overseeing governance, planning, risk management, change control, and reporting.

 

It ensures effective project controls and systems are in place across the entire project lifecycle, from development and delivery through to operations.

Required Majors: Engineering Fields and Computer Science.

Development

The Development stream focuses on conceiving, originating, planning, structuring, and overseeing all new project development initiatives.

 

This is where entrepreneurial flair and business fundamentals meet design, team-building, and high-level execution management.

Required Majors: Architecture Engineering and Real Estate.

Environment and Sustainability

The Environment and Sustainability stream aims to support the sustainable development and performance of the company through ensuring proper assessment of environmental impacts.

 

It ensures that development operations are carried out with minimal environmental impact, in addition to monitoring the implementation of these plans to ensure compliance with both company and international standards of environmental policies and procedures, local rules and regulations. The stream also covers the management of the social aspects of both Giga Projects.

Required Majors: Environment Engineering, Marine Science, Energy, Sustainability.

Special Projects & Engagement Activation

The Engagement Activation stream aims to enhance employee experience and foster a positive workplace culture. It manages internal events for Riyadh and onsite from planning and execution to vendor coordination, administers employee discounts, and organizes giveaways.

 

The department also oversees an internal events newsletter, and plans team-building activities. Additionally, it monitors engagement metrics and feedback to improve future initiatives. Moreover, the Special Projects stream is responsible for facilitating and supporting the BoD meetings, Project management, and adhoc requests.

Required Majors: Business Administration, Project Management, Industrial Engineering.

Requirements

Saudi Nationals

Saudi Nationals who graduated within the last two years 

Language skills

Native speakers in Arabic who are equally fluent in English in both verbal communication and writing 

Saudi Ministry of Education

Fresh graduates with a bachelor’s or master’s degree accredited by the Saudi Ministry of Education 

Pass

Completing and successfully passing the assessment and interviews

Find your passion

Successful applicants to the Elite Graduate Program will be working with an extraordinary team.

 

Working with the company does not only mean contributing to an organization – it is an investment in the future of Saudi Arabia, the future of the tourism industry and to global regeneration.
