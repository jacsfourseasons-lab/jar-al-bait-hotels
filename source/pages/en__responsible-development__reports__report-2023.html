Group Chief Executive Officer

Mr. John Pagano

Seeing our first guests marvel at the pristine natural environments we are fortunate to be custodians of, reinforces what I have always believed: that our natural environment is our greatest asset. We've gone beyond sustainability to rejuvenate natural habitats and conserve endangered species, achieving milestones that fill me with immense pride.

 

Our achievements in 2023 support ongoing efforts to achieve a net conservation gain of 30% across our destinations by 2040, and are transforming the way tourism development is delivered. Sharing our Annual Sustainability Report, ensures we remain transparent,  and demonstrates our efforts to continually rise to the challenge of finding new solutions to global issues.

2023

Key Highlights

Solar Energy

 

760,500 PV panels installed across The Red Sea’s expansive solar farms, driving our journey towards renewable energy.

Mangrove Conservation

 

600,000 mangrove seedlings planted, enhancing marine ecosystems and supporting biodiversity.

Coral Relocation

 

98,000 corals relocated to new habitats, preserving vital marine life.

Community Engagement

 

Enabled the potential reach of 3,000 local farms through the TAMALA cooperative.

Governance Excellence

 

Achieved re-certification for ISO 37000:2021 Governance of Organizations and ISO 31000:2018 Risk Management for RSG, including certification for four subsidiaries.

Setting New Standards

 

Secured the highest-ever score under the LEED v4.1 Cities and Communities certification, achieving Platinum with a record 96 points.

Looking Ahead

As we move forward, RSG remains dedicated to expanding its sustainability initiatives. In 2024, we aim to plant 1.5 million mangrove seedlings, further develop our renewable energy projects, and continue to enhance our waste management and water conservation efforts.

 

 

 

Our goal is to create a sustainable future that benefits both the environment and the communities we serve.
