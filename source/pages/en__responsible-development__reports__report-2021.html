2021

Key Highlights

Environmental Commitments

 

Dedicated to achieving 100% reliance on renewable energy, with 75% of islands designated as conservation zones.

Community Development

 

Implemented nearly 12,000 hours of training and hired 509 new employees, fostering local talent.

Governance

 

Established six new subsidiaries and implemented a comprehensive internal control toolkit.

Sustainable Finance

 

Secured the first Saudi Riyal-denominated Green Finance credit facility.

Global Recognition

 

Received first-stage Platinum LEED certification for Cities and Communities.

Looking Ahead

 

As we move forward, RSG remains committed to expanding its sustainability initiatives.

 

Our goals for 2022 and beyond include increasing renewable energy capacity, enhancing waste management practices, and furthering our community development programs.

 

We are dedicated to setting new benchmarks in regenerative tourism and sustainability.
