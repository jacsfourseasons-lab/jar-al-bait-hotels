OVERVIEW

Building Lasting Partnerships for Regeneration

At Red Sea Global (RSG), we collaborate with the world’s foremost organizations across sectors including transport, energy, construction, operations, technology, finance, media, sports, and conservation. We continually seeking new partners to join us in driving transformative change and sustainable development.

Our strategic alliances are integral to realizing the ambitions of Saudi Vision 2030, leveraging global expertise and cutting-edge solutions to unlock new avenues for economic growth and advancement. Together with our partners, we are shaping a future where progress and prosperity align seamlessly with environmental stewardship.

 

United by a shared commitment to regeneration, RSG and its partners are redefining the boundaries of responsible development creating extraordinary world-class destinations, fostering innovation, and generating social, environmental, and economic impact that lasts for generations.
