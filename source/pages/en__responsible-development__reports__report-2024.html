Group Chief Executive Officer

Mr. John Pagano

 

."2024 was a year of progress for our organization, with the opening of the first RSG-operated resorts, Shebara and Desert Rock, as well as Nujuma, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve. We welcomed thousands of guests to marvel at the beauty of the Red Sea coastline and opened the eyes of the world to the Saudi people’s rich culture and unrivaled hospitality.
Most importantly, we have achieved all of this in a way that protects and strengthens people and planet.
The concept of regenerative tourism – going one step further to actively enhance communities and natural habitats – is taking hold. That is down to the extraordinary ambition and talent of our team. 
Our people are inspiring positive change in the tourism and development sectors and putting Saudi Arabia at the forefront of the global sustainability conversation"

2024

Key Highlights

Emission Reduction

Avoided GHG emissions as a result of greater reliance on renewable energy, improvements in energy efficiency across operations, and the integration of sustainable practices within logistics.

Mangrove Restoration

3 million Mangrove seedlings produced as part of our Mangrove Compensation and Offset Program. The mangrove nursery also expanded its

production capacity to reach 2 million seedlings per year.

Community Engagement

Over 8,500 locals benefited from our capacity-building programs, which were facilitated through our award-winning Jewar app.

Economic Contribution

Contributed SAR 24 billion in 2024 to Saudi Arabia’s Gross Domestic Product, making it a significant part of the Kingdom’s growing tourism and hospitality industry. This impact is projected to increase as more resorts and destinations open to guests.

Looking Ahead

 

As we move forward, the next year is poised to be a pivotal one for RSG with the unveiling of residential offerings and the grand debut of Shura Island. As part of our ongoing commitments, we will continue to set new standards in emission reduction, renewable energy and waste management, redefining what it means to lead in responsible development.

 

 

With regenerative tourism at the forefront of our approach, we’re shaping our innovative and sustainable initiatives by going beyond merely protecting the natural environment and local communities to actively strengthening them.
