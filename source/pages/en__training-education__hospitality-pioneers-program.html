Hospitality Pioneers

From the heart of our destinations, redefining the kingdom's hospitality industry

Overview

Red Sea Global (RSG), in collaboration with the University of Tabuk’s College of Hospitality & Tourism in Al Wajh has launched the third edition of The Red Sea Hospitality Pioneers program.

 

Over 300 students have benefitted from this transformative program since 2022, aiming to train and employ talents local to the Red Sea region. Over the course of two years and six months, the program will provide dynamic training for future positions in hospitality, by providing a curriculum that combines theoretical knowledge with practical application.

Upon successful completion, talents will obtain an intermediate diploma from the University of Tabuk, accredited by Hong Kong Polytechnic University.

 

RSG and its partners will provide job opportunities to suitable and qualified graduates. Currently, 100 professional alumni from the program have already started their journey within our destinations, equipped with the necessary skills to thrive in the hospitality and tourism sectors.

Program Tracks

Diploma in Hospitality Management

Talents will gain knowledge and skills to help them succeed in careers and luxury hospitality services, encompassing all aspects from sales and customer service to implementing policies and procedures that ensure proper and smooth operations.

Diploma in Food and Beverage Management

Talents will learn a range of unique operational skills, including purchasing ingredients, developing menus, as well as preparing, presenting, and serving food and beverages.

Terms and Conditions

 Admission Criteria of Phase I (English Program)

 

Applicants must:

Saudi national

The applicant must be of Saudi nationality.

25

The applicant's age must not exceed 25 years and must not have graduated from high school more than (5) years ago.

Medical Certificate

Provide a medical certificate indicating that they are physically and mentally fit to work in the hospitality field.

Pass

The passing score for the achievement test and cognitive ability test must be 50% or higher.

Certification

Hold a high school diploma (as a full-time regular student) or its equivalent from either within the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia or abroad.

Full-time

Register as a full-time student and refrain from accepting enrollments into other programs or diploma, whether at the University of Tabuk or any other university.

Documents

Submit all required documents during the registration phase.

Completion

Ensure that all information provided in the application is accurate and complete.

Commitment letter

Sign a commitment letter issued by Red Sea Global to work in any of The Red Sea Global destinations for at least 3 years from the date of graduation.

How to Apply

1

Select your preferred track

Applicants will have the option to select one of the two tracks (Diploma in Hospitality Management or Diploma in Food and Beverage Management). The University of Tabuk and Red Sea Global will place applicants according to their selected preference and the availability of seats.

2

Send required documents

Enter the program information page and submit a new application on Tabuk University’s electronic admission portal. All required documents must be uploaded as requested. Applications missing any required documents will not be considered.

3

Interviews

Successful applications are determined according to complex admission criteria and in the order of preference, with available seats. All interviews will be conducted face-to-face in Al Wajh College of Tourism and Hospitality. Further details will be shared once candidates are selected.

4

Acceptance to the program

The applicant must follow the online portal to view the results of the nomination, as well as both the initial and final acceptance.
