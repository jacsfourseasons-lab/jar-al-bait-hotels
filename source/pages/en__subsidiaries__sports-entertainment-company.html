Red Sea Sports & Entertainment Company

Delivering unforgettable adventure, sport, and leisure experiences across The Red Sea destination 

Overview

RSSE manages a diverse portfolio of water-based, land-based, and marine adventure activities supporting guest engagement and leisure programming. Its operational framework ensures consistent safety standards, trained personnel, and experience governance.

 

The company collaborates with hospitality partners to integrate experiences into guest itineraries, contributing to the overall positioning of The Red Sea and AMAALA as leading destinations for sports and recreation. All activities are delivered in alignment with environmental and conservation policies.

FAQS

RSSE develops and manages a wide portfolio of sports, adventure, and leisure experiences for guests visiting The Red Sea and AMAALA destinations. 

The company offers a full range of above-water, below-water, and inland activities — from diving and sailing to hiking and mountain biking. 

They are RSSE’s specialist brands: WAMA (water sports and marine activities), Galaxea (diving and underwater exploration), and Akun (land-based adventure and cultural experiences). 

All activities follow strict international safety and training standards, led by certified professionals and environmental specialists. 

Every activity is designed to protect the natural environment, with special emphasis on coral preservation, waste reduction, and low-impact exploration. 

Guests can view and book experiences through the Visit Red Sea website at visitredsea.com/experiences/water-sportsopens in a new tab
