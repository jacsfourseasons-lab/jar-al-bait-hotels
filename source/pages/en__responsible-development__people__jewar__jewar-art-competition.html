Jewar Art Competition

Celebrating Saudi National Day 95

Overview

In celebration of the 95th Saudi National Day, Red Sea Global has launched the Jewar Art Competition, an open invitation to honor our authentic Saudi values and foster a sense of pride and national belonging through art and creativity.

Competition Guidelines

From the heart of our vibrant communities along the Red Sea and through Jewar platform, we proudly launch ‘Jewar Collective’. A unified platform that celebrates our national treasures: local artisans, creatives, and entrepreneurs.

 

Our aim is to nurture their authentic talents, open new economic horizons, and make their unique creations a window through which the world can experience Saudi Arabia’s rich heritage and inspiring stories.

 

This initiative directly supports RSG’s commitment to:

Applicants must be residents of the Red Sea regions

Submissions must be original and not created using AI

The artwork must be presented on a canvas or painting board

Each participant is allowed to submit only one artwork

The submission must align with the official identity of Saudi National Day 95
