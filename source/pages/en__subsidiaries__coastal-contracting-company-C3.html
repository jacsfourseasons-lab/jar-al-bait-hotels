Coastal Contracting Company (C3)

Delivering focused, high-quality construction solutions across Red Sea Global destinations

Overview

Coastal Contracting Company (C3) was established to meet upcoming construction requirements across Red Sea Global’s destinations and future external developments in the region. 

The company provides targeted execution of complex construction projects with an emphasis on quality, speed, and cost efficiency. C3’s in-house expertise ensures reliable project delivery and minimizes the risks often associated with third-party contractors. 

 

Created to support Red Sea Global’s growing infrastructure needs, C3 manages everything from building construction and MEP works to landscaping, modular fabrication, and asset leasing – maintaining flexibility to scale across diverse scopes while ensuring consistent standards of excellence.

Key Responsibilities & Services

Construction & Infrastructure

 

Manages substructure and superstructure concrete, façade, finishes, stormwater, sewage, potable water, firefighting systems, and telecom networks.

Logistics & Small Works

 

Oversees mobilization, temporary utilities, small projects (under SAR 10 million), and interiors fit-out, including mechanical, electrical, plumbing, and fire life safety works.

Temporary & Centralized Services

 

Provides temporary facilities for construction, centralized procurement, and leasing of construction equipment.

Landscaping & Golf Course Contracting

 

Handles landscaping, hardscaping, irrigation, water features, and golf course contracting — including site preparation, soil and grass testing, and shaping.

MEP

 

Construction of mechanical and electrical works, plumbing and drainage, fire life safety, and ELV systems.

Building & Infrastructure

 

Civil construction of buildings and infrastructure projects across all RSG assets and resort developments.

Landscape & Golf Contracting

 

Management of landscaping and golf course construction, including high-quality championship courses.

Modular

 

Alternative construction through light gauge steel buildings and pods manufactured in a controlled factory environment.

Asset Leasing

 

Leasing of construction assets and heavy machinery, from large tractors to small shovels, to support ongoing projects.
