Red Sea Fire & Rescue Services Company

Protecting lives and assets through world-class fire and rescue services

Overview

The Fire & Rescue Company delivers emergency response and fire safety operations across RSG developments. The subsidiary ensures preparedness, rapid response, and compliance with international fire and life safety standards.

Through continuous training, equipment maintenance, and inter-agency coordination, the company safeguards RSG’s guests, workforce, and infrastructure while maintaining consistent operational readiness.
