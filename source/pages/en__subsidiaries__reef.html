REEF

Environmental Regeneration Services Company - Efficient environmental solutions that restore land and sea ecosystems

Overview

REEF delivers terrestrial and marine regeneration programs, baseline environmental assessments, ecological monitoring, and long-term sustainability initiatives across RSG territories. The company plays a critical role in reducing third-party dependency and strengthening environmental governance

Its in-house capabilities support coral restoration, mangrove regeneration, and biodiversity protection—integrating environmental stewardship into destination development and daily operational activities.

Key Responsibilities & Services

In-house Environmental Service Provision

Reduce third-party reliance by conducting baseline surveys and enhancement projects internally for efficiency and cost-effectiveness. 

Cost Management & Optimization

Lower the cost of environmental services by building in-house capabilities and optimizing service delivery. 

Service Availability & Responsiveness

Ensure timely delivery of essential environmental services, including Environmental Management, Baseline Surveys, and Coral Management. 

Environmental Enhancement Programs

Manage mangrove plantation and enhancement projects, along with coral management initiatives, to improve ecosystem health. 

Terrestrial Regeneration

Restoration and recovery of land-based ecosystems that have been degraded, damaged, or destroyed.

Marine Regeneration

Restoration and enhancement of ocean and coastal ecosystems (e.g., coral and mangrove systems) that have been disturbed or degraded. 

Environmental Services

A broad range of professional activities aimed at protecting, managing, and enhancing the natural environment.
