A Vision Takes Shape

AMAALA is unveiled as an ultra‑luxury wellness destination on Saudi Arabia’s north‑western coast, conceived to redefine lifestyle, wellbeing, and cultural experiences. The destination is positioned as a cornerstone project supporting Saudi Vision 2030 and regenerative development ambitions.

Construction & Infrastructure

Development begins across the destination, with major infrastructure and enabling works underway. Over 600 construction and development contracts are awarded, advancing key components including Triple Bay, the marina, yacht club, and the wellness core.

Building Momentum

Significant construction milestones are achieved, including completed structural works for multiple resorts and advanced progress across marine and wellness assets. AMAALA confirms readiness for Phase One, marking a critical step toward welcoming its first guests.

Phase One Opens

AMAALA enters operations with the opening of Phase One, introducing a collection of world‑class resorts, the AMAALA Yacht Club, and the Corallium Marine Life Institute. Powered entirely by renewable energy, the destination sets a new global benchmark for regenerative luxury tourism.

Sustainability & Regeneration

AMAALA’s design and operations are guided by regenerative principles, ensuring nature and wellbeing coexist in perfect balance. Development is limited to less than 5% of the total area, with renewable energy and waste-free systems powering every experience. 

 

Through its wellness philosophy, ocean conservation, and community development initiatives, AMAALA demonstrates how tourism can enrich both people and planet.
